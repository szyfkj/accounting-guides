# Accounting guides for non-accountants

Plain-language guides to bookkeeping and business tax, written for owners, managers and staff who are not accountants.

## Start here

1. **[Accounting fundamentals](fundamentals/index.md)** explain the ideas every accounting system uses, in any country: the accounting equation, debits and credits, ledgers and financial statements.
2. **Country guides** cover the local law: who must keep books, the main taxes, filing deadlines and penalties.

## Country guides

More countries will be added over time. Use the language menu at the top of the page to switch languages.

!!! warning "Educational material only"
    These guides are not tax or legal advice. Rates, thresholds and deadlines change. Confirm current rules with the tax authority or a licensed accountant before you file or make a decision.
