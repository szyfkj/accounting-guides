# Penalties and common mistakes

Late tax costs money fast: unpaid tax carries a **surcharge of 1.5% per month** (up to the amount of the tax itself), on top of penalties and fines. Filing on time, even with no tax to pay, avoids most of it.

## Main penalties

| Problem | Consequence |
| --- | --- |
| Tax paid late | Surcharge of 1.5% per month or part month on the unpaid tax (Revenue Code Section 27) |
| Return filed late | Criminal fine (typically up to 2,000 baht per return), plus surcharge on any tax due |
| VAT under-declared or not filed | Penalty of up to 2 times the tax, reduced if you file voluntarily before the Revenue Department contacts you, plus surcharge |
| Tax invoice not issued, or issued without being VAT-registered | Penalty of 2 times the tax, plus fines |
| Tax not withheld, or withheld but not paid over | The payer becomes liable for the tax itself, plus surcharge; the expense may also be disallowed |
| Books not kept, or financial statements not filed with DBD | Fines on the company under the Accounting Act, and separate fines on its directors |

Amounts are set by law and can change; the Revenue Department publishes reduction schedules for voluntary late filing.

## Common mistakes non-accountants make

1. **Mixing personal and business money.** Use a separate company bank account. Personal spending through the company is disallowed for tax and can be treated as income to the director.
2. **Accepting purchase invoices with the wrong details.** A missing tax ID or wrong branch means no input VAT claim.
3. **Forgetting to withhold tax** on services and rent, then paying the tax yourself.
4. **Not collecting 50 Tawi certificates** from customers, so you cannot credit tax already withheld from you.
5. **Missing the VAT registration threshold.** Once revenue passes 1.8 million baht, you owe VAT on sales whether or not you charged it.
6. **Treating cash in the bank as profit.** Part of it is VAT, withheld tax and social security that belong to the government.
7. **Leaving everything to year-end.** Monthly bookkeeping and bank reconciliation make the audit faster and cheaper.
8. **Signing documents you do not understand.** Directors are personally liable for some filings, even when an accounting firm prepares them.
