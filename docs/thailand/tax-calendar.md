# Tax and compliance calendar

Most Thai filings are due on the **7th or 15th of each month**; e-filing generally adds 8 days. The 8-day e-filing extension currently runs until 31 January 2027 and may be renewed ([Nishimura & Asahi](https://www.nishimura.com/en/knowledge/publications/further-eight-day-extension-for-e-filing-tax-returns-and-online-tax-payments-in-thailand)). When a deadline falls on a weekend or public holiday, it moves to the next working day.

## Every month (for the previous month)

| Due (paper) | Due (e-filing) | Filing | Agency |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 7th | 15th | PND 1 – salary withholding | Revenue Department |
| 7th | 15th | PND 3, PND 53 – withholding on payments to individuals and companies | Revenue Department |
| 7th | 15th | PND 54, PP 36 – payments abroad (withholding and self-assessed VAT) | Revenue Department |
| 15th | 23rd | PP 30 – VAT return | Revenue Department |
| 15th | 23rd | PT 40 – Specific Business Tax | Revenue Department |
| 15th | 15th | สปส.1-10 – social security contributions | Social Security Office |

## Every year (company with a 31 December year-end)

| Due | Filing | Agency |
| --- | --- | --- |
| End of January | Workmen's Compensation Fund annual wage report and contribution | Social Security Office |
| End of February | PND 1 Kor – annual employee income summary; issue 50 Tawi to employees | Revenue Department |
| End of March | Signboard Tax return | Local authority |
| By 30 April | AGM approves audited financial statements (within 4 months of year-end) | Company |
| Within 14 days of AGM | บอจ.5 – shareholder list | DBD |
| Within 1 month of AGM | ส.บ.ช.3 – audited financial statements | DBD |
| Late May (150 days after year-end) | PND 50 – annual CIT return | Revenue Department |
| By 31 August | PND 51 – half-year CIT estimate | Revenue Department |
| Usually April–August | Land and Building Tax bill (paid when the local authority bills you) | Local authority |

A company with a different year-end shifts the annual dates to match. Put every date in a shared calendar with a reminder a week ahead.
