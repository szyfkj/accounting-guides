# Other taxes you may meet

Most small businesses deal mainly with CIT, VAT, withholding tax and payroll. These four others apply in specific situations.

| Tax | Who pays | Rate (main cases) | Filed with |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Specific Business Tax (SBT, ภาษีธุรกิจเฉพาะ) | Banks, finance companies, life insurers, pawnshops, and anyone selling real estate within 5 years of acquiring it | 3.3% (3% + 10% local tax) on gross receipts for most; 0.1% for pawnshops' sale of pledged goods | Revenue Department, form PT 40 (ภ.ธ.40), by the 15th of the next month |
| Stamp Duty (อากรแสตมป์) | Parties to certain documents | Leases and hire-of-work contracts: 1 baht per 1,000 baht (0.1%); loans: 0.05%, capped at 10,000 baht; share transfers: 0.1% | Revenue Department, mostly by e-Stamp Duty within 15 days of signing |
| Land and Building Tax (ภาษีที่ดินและสิ่งปลูกสร้าง) | Owners of land and buildings | Varies by use: agricultural, residential, commercial, or vacant; commercial use starts at 0.3% of appraised value | Local authority (district office or municipality), billed each year |
| Signboard Tax (ภาษีป้าย) | Owners of business signs | Based on sign size and whether it uses Thai, foreign, or mixed text | Local authority, return (ภ.ป.1) by the end of March |

Sale of real estate subject to SBT is exempt from VAT, and when SBT applies, stamp duty on the transfer does not. Importers also pay customs duty and import VAT at the border, and some goods (alcohol, tobacco, fuel, cars) carry excise tax.
