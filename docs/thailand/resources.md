# Where to get help

Official agencies publish forms, rates and deadlines; check them before every filing season.

| Agency | Responsible for | Website | Hotline |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

| Department of Business Development (กรมพัฒนาธุรกิจการค้า) | Company registration, bookkeeper rules, financial statement filing | [dbd.go.th](https://www.dbd.go.th) | 1570 |

## When to bring in an accountant

Most companies use an outside accounting firm for bookkeeping and monthly filings, and must use a licensed auditor every year. Get professional advice before you:

- Set up the company, choose its year-end, or decide whether to register for VAT voluntarily
- Pay anyone overseas (treaty rates, PND 54, PP 36)
- Buy, sell or rent property
- Hire your first employee
- Apply for BOI promotion or other tax incentives
- Receive a letter or audit notice from the Revenue Department

Use this guide to ask better questions, and to check the work you receive.
