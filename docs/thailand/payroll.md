# Payroll: personal income tax and social security

An employer must withhold personal income tax from salaries every month, pay social security contributions for both sides, and file returns with two agencies: the Revenue Department and the Social Security Office (SSO, สำนักงานประกันสังคม).

## Personal income tax rates

The tax is progressive: each slice of net income is taxed at its own rate.

| Net taxable income (baht per year) | Rate |
| --- | --- |
| 0 – 150,000 | 0% |
| 150,001 – 300,000 | 5% |
| 300,001 – 500,000 | 10% |
| 500,001 – 750,000 | 15% |
| 750,001 – 1,000,000 | 20% |
| 1,000,001 – 2,000,000 | 25% |
| 2,000,001 – 5,000,000 | 30% |
| Over 5,000,000 | 35% |

Source: [PwC Worldwide Tax Summaries](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/thailand/individual/taxes-on-personal-income).

**Net taxable income** = salary for the year − employment expense deduction (50% of salary, capped at 100,000 baht) − personal allowances. The basic allowance is 60,000 baht for the taxpayer; others cover a spouse, children, parents, social security contributions, life insurance, provident fund and more.

Example: an employee earns 30,000 baht a month (360,000 a year) and claims only the basic allowance and social security (875 × 12 = 10,500).

| Step | Baht |
| --- | --- |
| Annual salary | 360,000 |
| Less expense deduction (50%, max 100,000) | (100,000) |
| Less personal allowance | (60,000) |
| Less social security | (10,500) |
| **Net taxable income** | **189,500** |
| Tax: first 150,000 at 0%, next 39,500 at 5% | **1,975 a year (about 165 a month)** |

The employer estimates the annual tax each month and withholds one twelfth.

## Employer filings for income tax

| Form | What | When |
| --- | --- | --- |
| PND 1 (ภ.ง.ด.1) | Monthly tax withheld from salaries | By the 7th of the next month (15th e-filed) |
| PND 1 Kor (ภ.ง.ด.1ก) | Annual summary of each employee's income and tax | By the end of February |
| 50 Tawi to each employee | Annual certificate of income and tax withheld | Early in the new year, so staff can file their own return |

Employees then file their own annual return, **PND 91 (ภ.ง.ด.91)**, by 31 March (8 days later if e-filed), and claim any refund.

## Social Security Fund (กองทุนประกันสังคม)

- Employer and employee each pay **5% of monthly wages**.
- From 1 January 2026, the wage base is a minimum of 1,650 and a maximum of **17,500 baht**, so the maximum contribution is **875 baht** per side per month (it was 750). The ceiling rises to 20,000 baht in 2029 and 23,000 baht in 2032. ([Baker McKenzie](https://www.bakermckenzie.com/en/insight/publications/alerts/2025/12/thailand-higher-wage-base-for-ssf-contributions))
- Register new employees with the SSO within 30 days of hiring.
- File and pay contributions (form สปส.1-10) by the **15th of the following month**.

Employers also pay a small annual contribution to the **Workmen's Compensation Fund** (กองทุนเงินทดแทน), which covers work injuries; the rate depends on the type of business.
