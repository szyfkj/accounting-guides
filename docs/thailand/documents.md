# Documents: tax invoices, receipts and e-Tax invoices

In Thailand, a deduction or VAT claim is only as good as the document behind it. Wrong or missing details are the most common reason an expense is disallowed in a tax audit.

## Common business documents

| Document | Thai | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Quotation | ใบเสนอราคา | Offer of price; no accounting entry |
| Invoice / billing note | ใบแจ้งหนี้ / ใบวางบิล | Asks the customer to pay |
| Tax invoice | ใบกำกับภาษี | Shows the VAT charged; the buyer's evidence for claiming input VAT |
| Receipt | ใบเสร็จรับเงิน | Proves payment was received |
| Credit note / debit note | ใบลดหนี้ / ใบเพิ่มหนี้ | Reduces or increases an earlier tax invoice (returns, discounts, price changes) |
| Withholding tax certificate | 50 ทวิ | Proves tax was withheld (see [withholding tax](withholding-tax.md)) |
| Substitute receipt | ใบรับรองแทนใบเสร็จ | Signed by an employee when a small supplier, such as a taxi or market stall, cannot give a receipt |

Thai businesses often combine documents into one form, such as "tax invoice/receipt" (ใบกำกับภาษี/ใบเสร็จรับเงิน).

## What a full tax invoice must show

Under Section 86/4 of the Revenue Code, a full tax invoice must include:

- [ ] The words "Tax Invoice" (ใบกำกับภาษี) clearly displayed
- [ ] Seller's name, address and 13-digit tax ID
- [ ] Buyer's name, address and tax ID (for a VAT-registered buyer)
- [ ] Head office or branch number of the seller and buyer (สำนักงานใหญ่ or สาขาที่ 00001)
- [ ] A serial number for the invoice (and the book number, if used)
- [ ] Name, type, quantity and value of the goods or services
- [ ] VAT amount shown separately from the price
- [ ] Date of issue

Use this list to check purchase invoices before you pay them. Ask the supplier to reissue any invoice with the wrong company name, address or branch.

## When to issue the tax invoice

The tax invoice is issued when the **tax point** arises, which also fixes the month the VAT belongs to:

- **Goods:** on delivery, transfer of ownership, payment or invoice issue, whichever comes first.
- **Services:** when payment is received, or an earlier tax invoice is issued.

## Abbreviated tax invoice

Retailers may issue an abbreviated tax invoice (ใบกำกับภาษีแบบย่อ), typically from a POS machine registered with the Revenue Department. The buyer **cannot** claim input VAT on it. If you buy for the company, ask for a full tax invoice.

## e-Tax invoice and e-Receipt

The Revenue Department's [e-Tax Invoice & e-Receipt](https://etax.rd.go.th) system lets businesses issue these documents electronically instead of on paper. Small businesses can use the "by email" route, while larger ones send data directly. Issuing and receiving e-documents is not yet mandatory, but it saves printing and storage, and buyers can deduct some e-Tax expenses at a higher rate under current incentives.
