# The accounting equation

Every accounting system rests on one equation, and it must always balance:

$$
\text{Assets} = \text{Liabilities} + \text{Equity}
$$

In plain words: everything the business owns was paid for either with borrowed money (liabilities) or with the owners' money (equity).

## The five account types

Every account in the books belongs to one of five types.

| Type | What it means | Examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Assets | What the business owns or is owed | Cash, bank deposits, accounts receivable, inventory, vehicles, computers |
| Liabilities | What the business owes others | Accounts payable, bank loans, VAT payable, withholding tax payable, accrued salaries |
| Equity | The owners' stake | Paid-up share capital, retained earnings |
| Income (revenue) | Value earned from customers | Sales, service fees, interest received |
| Expenses | Costs used up to earn income | Cost of goods sold, rent, salaries, electricity, depreciation |

Income and expenses feed equity. Profit (income minus expenses) increases what the owners have in the business; a loss reduces it. So the expanded equation is:

$$
\text{Assets} = \text{Liabilities} + \text{Equity} + (\text{Income} - \text{Expenses})
$$

## A simple example

Somchai starts a coffee shop company.

1. Shareholders put in 1,000,000 baht. Assets (bank) +1,000,000; Equity (share capital) +1,000,000.
2. The company borrows 500,000 baht from a bank. Assets (bank) +500,000; Liabilities (loan) +500,000.
3. It buys a coffee machine for 300,000 baht. Assets (bank) −300,000; Assets (equipment) +300,000. Total assets do not change.
4. It sells coffee for 50,000 baht in cash. Assets (cash) +50,000; Income +50,000.
5. It pays rent of 20,000 baht. Assets (bank) −20,000; Expenses +20,000.

After these five events: assets are 1,530,000 baht, liabilities 500,000, equity 1,000,000, and profit 30,000. 1,530,000 = 500,000 + 1,000,000 + 30,000. The equation balances, as it always must.
